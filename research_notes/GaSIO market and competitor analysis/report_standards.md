# Industry-standard names, formats and frameworks for a combined Technology + Market + Competitor + Novelty report (cleantech hardware, GaSIO / Vietnam biomass gasification)

Note: direct fetches to lek.com, icaew.com, businessfinland.fi were blocked by the egress proxy; findings marked "(search summary)" are from search summaries of the cited pages, not full-text reads.

## Q1. What are the standard report names, and how do they differ?

### Takeaway
No single name covers all four parts. In investment practice the closest label is **integrated Commercial and Technical (Technology) Due Diligence**: CDD covers market, competitors and customers; TDD covers technology, maturity and scalability. Outside a transaction, the same content is usually called a **"Technology & Market Assessment"** (Lux Research: "Market or Tech Assessment"). For industrial projects aimed at development funders, the long-standing label is a **(pre-)feasibility study** (UNIDO manual). Novelty is assessed separately in IP documents: patentability / prior-art search, FTO, patent landscape.

### Cited Findings
**Commercial Due Diligence (CDD)**
- CDD asks whether the company is solid within an attractive market: market size and trajectory, competitive position, customer loyalty/value, whether the growth case behind the price holds; may include price/volume forecasts. — [ICAEW CDD Guideline](https://www.icaew.com/-/media/corporate/files/technical/corporate-finance/guidelines/icaew-commercial-due-diligence-guideline.ashx); [KPMG Ireland CDD guideline](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/ie/pdf/services/deal-advisory/ie-commercial-due-diligence-guideline.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf) (search summary)
- ICAEW publishes a formal CDD guideline → recognized professional service category in UK corporate finance. — [ICAEW](https://www.icaew.com/-/media/corporate/files/technical/corporate-finance/guidelines/icaew-commercial-due-diligence-guideline.ashx)

**Technical / Technology Due Diligence (TDD)**
- For industrial/real-asset deals, TDD assesses asset condition, fitness for current/planned use, asset and site risks, possibly forecast opex/capex. — [Gesvalt](https://gesvalt.es/en/companies/consultancy/technic-due-diligence/) (search summary)
- RICS "Technical Due Diligence of Commercial Property" has a section on report layout/contents → TDD is a formal deliverable type. — [RICS](https://rics.org/content/dam/ricsglobal/documents/standards/Technical_Due_Diligence_Of_Commercial_Property_1st_Edition.pdf)
- L.E.K. argues investors need **integrated** commercial and technology DD (can the architecture scale, does the roadmap fit the thesis; technical findings feed the commercial thesis). — [L.E.K.](https://www.lek.com/insights/technology/why-investors-need-integrated-commercial-and-technology-due-diligence) (search summary)

**Technology & Market Assessment (non-transactional)**
- Lux Research "Market or Tech Assessments": "assessing market and best-positioned technology opportunities by crystallizing unmet needs, charting industry and consumer trends, and evaluating commercial readiness." Lux Innovation Grid scores technical value, business execution, maturity. — [Lux Research](https://luxresearchinc.com/?p=2160); [Lux/Eneco client story 2026](https://luxresearchinc.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Lux-Research-Eneco-Client-Story.pdf) (search summary)
- Frost & Sullivan rates technologies on R&D footprint, IP/patenting, funding, market potential. — [Frost & Sullivan TCP](https://www.frost.com/?p=32321); [frost.com](https://frost.com/?p=49177) (search summary)
- Tech-transfer centres offer "technology and market intelligence" as a named service. — [UHCL TMAC](https://www.uhcl.edu/tmac/services/technology-and-market-intelligence)

**Feasibility Study (UNIDO)**
- UNIDO *Manual for the Preparation of Industrial Feasibility Studies* (1978; Behrens & Hawranek revised ed. 1991): market research, raw materials, engineering & technology (incl. choice of technology), financial analysis, investment appraisal. ToC: executive summary → project background/history → market and plant capacity → ... — [UNIDO 1978 PDF](https://downloads.unido.org/ot/47/90/4790657/00001-10000_07534.pdf); [1991 edition](https://old.wzr.ug.edu.pl/anna-wojewnik-filipkowska/upload/files/BEHRENS%20HAWRANEK%20manual_for_the_preparation_of_industrial_feasibility_studies.pdf); [UNIDO article](https://unido.org/node/4222950) (search summary)

**IP-based novelty documents**
- **Patentability (novelty) search** tests whether the invention is new and non-obvious against prior disclosures. **FTO** is "strictly defensive", territory-specific, "about a product, not an invention"; a granted patent does not give FTO; FTO opinion often "substantially more costly" than the search. — [Spruson & Ferguson](https://www.spruson.com/understanding-the-differences-between-patentability-novelty-and-freedom-to-operate-prior-art-searching/); [Hindles](https://www.hindles.co.uk/freedom-to-operate); [TT Consultants](https://ttconsultants.com/navigating-the-intellectual-property-landscape-patentability-vs-freedom-to-operate-fto-search/)
- **Patent Landscape Report:** WIPO *Guidelines for Preparing Patent Landscape Reports* (WIPO/PUB/946, 2015, Anthony Trippe) set a quality standard. — [WIPO PUB 946](https://tind.wipo.int/record/28858/files/wipo_pub_946.pdf); [IGE](https://www.ige.ch/en/services/newsroom/news/news-details/wipos-guidelines-for-preparing-patent-landscape-reports-the-basis-for-patent-landscape-analyses)
- WIPO CDIP: PLRs show "what is actually being patented, where and by whom". — [WIPO CDIP/4/6](https://www.wipo.org/edocs/mdocs/mdocs/en/cdip_4/cdip_4_6.pdf)

### Inferences
- CDD/TDD are commissioned by investors *on* a company; a company's own document is usually titled "Technology & Market Assessment", "Investment Memorandum" or "Business Plan", with CDD/TDD-style structure.
- Competitive Landscape Analysis, Market Study, Go-to-Market Strategy and TEA are normally chapters/annexes, not umbrella standards.

## Q2. What do climate-hardware investors and grant funders expect?

### Takeaway
Climate-hardware investors look at TRL, scale-up risk and a credible path to cost competitiveness (TEA); often unwilling to take market risk. **EIC Accelerator** scores Excellence (novelty/breakthrough, timing, feasibility, IP/FTO), Impact (scale-up, market), Risk/need for EU support; requires a **Freedom to Operate Analysis annex**. **GCF** requires feasibility / pre-feasibility studies plus economic & financial analysis annex. **Spark/Spark+** (Clean Cooking Alliance) look for commercially viable, post-proof-of-concept, scalable enterprises with an investor-ready DD data room.

### Cited Findings
- Investors use NASA-derived TRLs; credible route to cost competitiveness expected; TEAs key. — [Verde checklist](https://blog.verde.ag/en/due-diligence-climatech-investments/); [Planet A Technology & Product DD](https://planet-a.notion.site/1-Technology-Product-8ce55c45b1d64bb9ae5b8771462902d9)
- Impact VC splits scale-up, technology and market risk; accepts more scale-up/tech risk, not market risk. — [Impact VC interview](https://impactvc.substack.com/p/investing-in-hardware-interview-with) (search summary)
- Spark Fund: market-based, commercially viable, past proof of concept, generating income, scalable; grants up to $300k (Venture) / $500k (Growth); in-depth DD. — [Spark Fund III RFP](https://cleancooking.org/funding-opps/request-for-proposals-spark-fund-iii-rfp-14-4/)
- Spark+ targets biomass, LPG and other stove/fuel businesses, debt/equity ready in ~12–18 months; pitch materials, financial model, DD data room. — [CCA Spark+](https://cleancooking.org/?p=1279) (search summary). Spark+ Africa Fund targets $50M (Sub-Saharan Africa). — [DFC](https://ewsdata.rightsindevelopment.org/files/documents/AF/DFC-2021-SPARKAFRICAF.pdf)
- EIC criteria and annexes (financial plan, **Freedom to Operate Analysis**, data management plan, LOIs, CVs). — [LT EIC info day](https://lino.lmt.lt/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/EIC-Info-day-LT-Project-selection-and-expectations-to-the-applicant_Vilma-Radvilaite_final.pdf); [PNO Innovation](https://www.pnoinnovation.com/insights/key-insights-on-eic-accelerator/)
- EIC targets ~TRL 6→8; TRL 5 validation expected. — [Innovation Israel](https://innovationisrael.org.il/iserd/eic-accelerator/)
- BayFOR recommends TAM/SAM/SOM, USP comparison, competitor analysis, SWOT for EIC. — [BayFOR](https://www.bayfor.org/fileadmin/user_upload/BayFOR-pdfs/aktuelles/veranstaltungen/BayFOR-eic-accelerator-full-application-public-natalia-garcia-mozo.pdf)
- GCF: proposal ≤60 pages, annexes incl. feasibility studies; SAP Annex 2 pre-feasibility study; Annex 11 economic & financial analysis. — [GCF FP template](https://www.greenclimate.fund/sites/default/files/document/form-fp-template-pap.docx); [GCF SAP Annex 2](https://www.greenclimate.fund/document/guidance-preparing-pre-feasibility-study-annex-2-under-sap); [GCF Annex 11](https://www.greenclimate.fund/document/guidelines-preparing-economic-and-financial-analysis-annex-11-pap-proposals)

### Inferences
- GaSIO would reach GCF only via an Accredited Entity programme.
- Portable package: (1) Technology & Market Assessment in CDD/TDD logic, (2) TRL + CRI with evidence, (3) TEA vs LPG/coal/firewood, (4) FTO/patentability memo, (5) ISO 19867 results + carbon methodology note.

## Q3. Frameworks and standards used inside such reports

- EU TRL 1–9 (H2020 Annex G, carried into Horizon Europe). — [EC H2020 Annex G](https://ec.europa.eu/research/participants/data/ref/h2020/wp/2014_2015/annexes/h2020-wp1415-annex-g-trl_en.pdf)
- ISO 16290:2013 (space TRL definitions, usable more widely; confirmed 2024). — [ISO](https://www.iso.org/standard/56064.html)
- ARENA Commercial Readiness Index (CRI 1–6), complements TRL. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commercial_readiness_index); [Carbon Trust](https://www.carbontrust.com/our-work-and-impact/guides-reports-and-tools/assessing-the-commercial-maturity-of-renewable-energy-technologies-moving-beyond-technology-readiness)
- Global CO2 Initiative TEA & LCA Guidelines (2018; v2.0). — [Deep Blue v2.0](https://deepblue.lib.umich.edu/items/b1432c18-29e4-4514-8c77-45f2be052ac9)
- ISO 56005:2020 IP management guidance. — [ISO](https://www.iso.org/standard/72761.html)
- ISO/TR 19867-3:2018 voluntary tiers (Tier 0–5 for thermal efficiency, PM2.5, CO, safety, durability), informative only; Tier 5 PM2.5/CO most likely to meet WHO guidelines. — [ISO](https://www.iso.org/standard/73935.html); [CCA](https://cleancooking.org/news/10-16-2018-voluntary-performance-targets/); [WHO](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240023987)
- Verra VMR0006 (26% discount with CDM Tool 30 fNRB) — **replaced by VM0050 on 9 Oct 2024, mandatory from 2027 vintage**. — [Verra](https://verra.org/methodologies/vmr0006-methodology-for-installation-of-high-efficiency-firewood-cookstoves/)
- Standard but not primary-verified here: Porter's Five Forces, PESTEL, SWOT, TAM/SAM/SOM, ISO 14040/14044 LCA.

## Q5. How novelty is formally assessed
- PCT Art. 33: novelty (not anticipated by prior art), inventive step (not obvious to a person skilled in the art), industrial applicability; non-binding preliminary opinion. — [WIPO PCT Art. 33](https://www.wipo.int/pct/en/texts/articles/a33.html); [WIPO ISPE Guidelines ch. 17](https://www.wipo.int/pct/en/texts/ispe/17_01_03.html)
- A patent gives control over its claims, not freedom from third-party patents. — [Spruson & Ferguson](https://www.spruson.com/understanding-the-differences-between-patentability-novelty-and-freedom-to-operate-prior-art-searching/)
- Inference: split novelty into (a) legal novelty (search/FTO, VN/PCT filing status), (b) technical state of the art (TLUD, forced-draft gasifier stoves, industrial gasifier burners), (c) performance edge (KPI benchmark matrix: ISO 19867-3 tiers, efficiency, fuel flexibility, cost per useful MJ, durability, price, service). Avoid "novel" in the patent sense without an attorney search.

## Recommended name and structure (synthesis)
**Title:** "GaSIO Technology & Market Assessment — Technology Due Diligence, Market & Competitive Landscape, and Technology-Edge Analysis". Alternative cover: "Technology and Commercial Due Diligence Report (Vendor-prepared)".

1. Executive summary (edge verdict, TRL/CRI, market size, key risks)
2. Company & product overview
3. Technology deep-dive (TDD): principle, fuel flexibility, manufacturing/scale-up, TRL per line, CRI, ISO 19867 tiers, durability/safety
4. State of the art & novelty: mini patent landscape (WIPO PLR style), patentability/FTO status (PCT Art. 33), IP strategy (ISO 56005)
5. Market analysis (CDD): TAM/SAM/SOM, PESTEL, segments, willingness to pay
6. Competitive landscape: KPI benchmark matrix, Five Forces, positioning map
7. Technology-edge assessment: KPI benchmarking + TEA (cost per meal / LCOH, payback)
8. Impact & carbon: VM0050 / Gold Standard; WHO-linked tiers; optional LCA
9. Go-to-market & scale-up
10. Risk register
11. SWOT, conclusions, funder-fit matrix (EIC, Spark+, climate VCs, strategic partners)
12. Annexes: test reports, TRL evidence, FTO memo, TEA/financial model, sources
