## Technology claims (GaSIO = VCBG, "khí hóa sinh khối liên tục theo thể tích" = volumetric continuous biomass gasification)
- Gasification under oxygen-limited conditions; two products: syngas (burned for heat) and biochar (soil improvement, environmental cleanup).
- Gasifies all locally available biomass; process not interrupted because feed is continuous; minimalist design.
- Max efficiency: household stove 83.7%; industrial devices up to 73%.
- Savings: up to 40% fuel cost vs coal, 80% vs LPG or oil (device-dependent). Fuel page: 54% vs coal, 78% vs diesel, 77% vs gas.
- Safety: "no toxic substances produced", emissions meet international standards.
- Vietnam residues: 160 Mt total, ~65 Mt burned/discarded, potential 24 Mtoe (~USD 28 bn) (2022). ~75% of fuel consumed in Vietnam is fossil.
- 1,027 kg CO2 reduced per year per gasifier stove (overview) — but flyers say 3–5 t/yr (household) and 4.5–7.5 t/yr (service stove); lifetime 8 t (overview) vs 30–50 t (flyer) — INTERNAL INCONSISTENCY.
- Biochar sells internationally ~8M VND/t, 4–5x the biomass input.
- Fuels: rice husk, sawdust, wood chips, corn cobs, peanut shells, chopped cinnamon branches, wood shavings, pellets.
